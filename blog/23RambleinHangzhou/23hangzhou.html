Summertime Citywalk in Hangzhou

Summer 2023 in Hangzhou, walked with the camera tilted slightly upwards: modern façades, and the cumulus clouds that seem to park themselves right on the rooftops.

Trip Notes
Place Hangzhou, China
What we saw Planted façades · a campus running track · a painted street block left over from an old industrial area
A planted façade: greenery in the balcony troughs, clouds behind it.
Above: a white-roofed pavilion seen through the trees. Below: an overhanging roof on turquoise trusses.
Above: a campus running track framed by leaves. Below: the same track from under the canopy.
Lanes painted red and blue on an old street block — the wall still reads CIAO UNCLE.
