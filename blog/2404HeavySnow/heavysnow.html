Late April · Snow · Birds

At the end of April 2024 it snowed heavily again on the Aalto University campus. Spring had already started — one half of the frame was meltwater and open sea, the other half was still winter, and the birds landed in between.

Trip Notes
Place Aalto University campus, Espoo, Finland
What we saw Snow loading the branches · mallards back at the pond · gulls and wintering waterfowl on a half-frozen shore
A great tit standing its ground in the snow — the residents never left.
Mallards back at the pond, resting on a bank of snow.
