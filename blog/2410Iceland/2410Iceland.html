Road Trip in South Iceland

A photo log from the fieldwork excursion we made for the Turfhouse project in Iceland, 2024 — the turf houses we came to study, and the south-coast road between them. Two things the camera kept coming back to: the northern lights, and a mountain ridge across the bay turning gold at sunset.

Trip Notes
Place Reykjavík & the South Iceland ring route
Related project Intestine Architecture 🥧 — the Turfhouse VR project this fieldwork fed
What we saw Northern lights · turf houses under their grass roofs · a ridge lit gold across the bay · a two-tier waterfall in a rift valley
The ridge on the far side of the bay holding the last of the evening light.
Same ridge minutes later: open sea, then a long wall of mountain.
Hallgrímskirkja — Reykjavík’s landmark: a stepped concrete spire rising over the statue in front of it.
Corrugated steel and white frames; the dried flowers are still in the window boxes.
A snow-capped mountain standing over the autumn birch, wrapped bales in the field below.
A turf house by the coast: timber gable, mossy stone wall, and a roof that is simply grass.
The turf farm from the meadow — gable after gable under one continuous roof of grass.
Autumn on the hillside behind the farm: one red roof, one turf roof, and the whole slope turning.
A two-tier waterfall cutting through a rift valley, seen from the upper path.
The ground layer, close up: pale lichen fanned out beside a rosette of green.
