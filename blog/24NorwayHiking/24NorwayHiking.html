From the Fjords to the Trails

A summer of walking in Norway, 2024: Oslo, a village at the head of a fjord, Bergen, and then south to the Lysefjord for the two rocks every photograph of this country seems to come from. Sixteen frames, in the order they were taken.

Trip Notes
Route Oslo · Flåm · Bergen · Lysefjord
Landmarks Preikestolen (Pulpit Rock) · Kjeragbolten · Bryggen
What we saw Fjord walls and waterfalls · wooden towns · two summit hikes · a working coast
Havnelageret, the “pink palace” of Bjørvika — a harbour warehouse that really is pink.
Monica Bonvicini’s She Lies, floating off the Opera House: steel and glass, ice on the water.
nch’s The Scream, behind glass in Oslo.
Flåm: Fretheim Hotel, a timber house at the head of the valley.

One of the Fløyen goats, wearing its virtual-fence collar.

A farm down in the valley: a few green fields cut out of the forest, and one red roof.
