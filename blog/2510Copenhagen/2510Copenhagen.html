Harbour and Deer Park

Two days in Copenhagen, 2025. The first stayed on the water: Nyhavn, the harbour front opposite the Opera House, the Little Mermaid, the old citadel. The second went north to the deer park at Klampenborg, where the beech was turning and the red deer were out on the open plain.
