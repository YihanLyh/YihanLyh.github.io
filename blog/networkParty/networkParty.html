Network Party Internship 🎨 — MR plaza experience & interactive installation (2022)
Bottom line

This 2022 internship at Network Party ran on two threads. One was the mixed-reality experience for a city plaza: I shot the site in 360° with an Insta360, ran remote scene tests together with the development team, then re-adapted the whale digital model for MR rendering and animated it, pushing the digitisation work forward. The other was a mall interactive-installation and cultural-exhibition digitisation proposal — the Play Me I'm Yours piano-light interactive piece — where my main job was the installation model's rendering and animation. This experience went on to push me, later in graduate school, to study design methods and the technology behind real-world implementation, and to keep exploring and applying new tools.

Outcome lead

A 2022 spatial-and-interaction internship at Network Party, mainly on two projects: a mixed-reality whale spatial-experience piece for a city plaza, and an interactive-light installation proposal for a shopping mall.

01Plaza MR — the Whale

Capture the place, then anchor the model in it

The whale is a real spatial installation — a piece that had already landed in Beijing. My part of the internship was its MR-facing layer: I shot the site in 360° with an Insta360, ran remote scene tests with the development team, then re-adapted the whale digital model for MR rendering and animated it to prototype the MR visual and interaction effects, pushing the digitisation forward. At that stage this wasn't yet a deployable MR product: it was an early step that made the original architecture more digital, and the prototype was viewed on a mobile device rather than a headset.

Working with the development team. This MR exploration ran on Nreal's Cloud XDK — a Unity SDK that reconstructs the captured environment as a point cloud and localises the device against it, so a digital model can be placed into the real plaza and move through it. For me at the time it was an exploration on the development side; the prototype was finally viewed on a mobile device.

02Mall Installation & Exhibition Proposal

From one creature to a whole venue

The second deliverable widened the lens from one creature to a whole venue. We proposed digitising a shopping mall's cultural programming through interactive installation — including Play Me I'm Yours, a piano whose keys light up to invite people to play. My main contribution was the installation animation: making the piece move the way an invitation to play should feel.
